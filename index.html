Who I Am

I’m an Urban & Regional Planning student at Flinders University and Treasurer of the Flinders Urban & Regional Planning Collective. With hands-on experience in transport & logistics, guest experience at Adelaide Oval, and a deep love for how cities work, I bring practical insight to complex planning challenges.

Beyond the professional world, I create content on YouTube, make music (including a full album), and love public speaking. I’m an avid road cyclist — chasing hills, exploring Adelaide’s suburbs and beyond, and diving into the data behind every ride. Living in Adelaide gives me a front-row seat to the challenges and opportunities shaping Australia’s cities.
